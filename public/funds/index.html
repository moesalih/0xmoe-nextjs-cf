FUNDS
A Simple and Fun Budget Calculator
DOWNLOAD

Creating a budget should be easy and fun. Funds helps you create a budget by simplifying the process and eliminating complicated steps. Simply add your income and expenses including the amount and frequency (daily, weekly, monthly, yearly) for each item and Funds will calculate and visualize how much you're spending and saving. The color scheme will also change based on how much you're saving to give you a quick impression of your savings throughout the app.
